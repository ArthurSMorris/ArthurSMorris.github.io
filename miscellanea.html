Miscellanea

A place for things that do not fit naturally on a research CV.

Sport

I enjoy sport outside work. This section is intentionally left general because my CV does not specify particular sports; I can replace it with a more personal paragraph once I decide what I want to include publicly.

Science communication

I was runner-up in the 2023 East-Anglia heats of the Institute of Physics Three Minute Wonder competition.

As the Physics subject ambassador for Corpus Christi College, Oxford, I helped with open days and interviews and spoke with prospective applicants about studying physics.

Across disciplines

Before specialising in topological matter and quantum information, I worked on projects spanning accelerator physics, fluid dynamics and computational biology. I like keeping some contact with ideas outside my immediate field.

Places

Research has taken me through Oxford, Cambridge, Copenhagen, Berkeley, Tokyo and Marseille, as well as shorter schools and visits elsewhere. I have found that changing research environments is often a useful way to change how I think about a problem.
