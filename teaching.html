Teaching approach

I like to make the structure of a problem visible before concentrating on calculation. In mathematical physics this often means moving between a concrete example, a diagram and the compact symbolic form — for example, understanding what an expression such as \(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E}=\rho/\varepsilon_0\) is saying before manipulating it.
